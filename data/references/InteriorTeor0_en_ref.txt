Application for the uniformization of interpretation of law (Turma) No 0002829-74.2023.4.05.8308/ PE

Federal Judge Leonardo Castanho Mendes

National Institute of Social Security - INSS

Requested by: Maria de Lourdes Lucas Macedo

Reported

The incident of the INSS invests against the judgment of the 3rd Recursal Tower of Pernambuco which, in denying provision to its appeal, recognised the quality of special insurance in favour of the applicant, although she is a beneficiary of death pension at a value above the minimum. There is a violation of the provisions of article 11, § 9o, I, of Law 8.213/91, included by Law 11.718/08.

Inadmissed the incident in the origin, it passed agravely, provided by the decision of the Presidency of this Turma.

I reported.

Voting

While the appealed judgment considered irrelevant, in order to remove the quality of insurance from the applicant, the fact that she is the beneficiary of death pension of a value greater than the lowest benefit of continued benefit of the Social Security, the paradigm judgments, in applying the provisions of article 11, § 9, I, of Law 8.213/91, with the draft given by Law 11.718/08, removed the condition of special insurance of the applicants due to the death pension perceived to be of a value greater than the minimum.

The incident must also be provided, in accordance with what was decided by this Turma in the PUIL 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304 judgment, in the virtual session held in the period from 29/08/24 to 04/09/24.

This is how the winning vote was drawn up by the reporter, the Federal Judge Caio Moysés de Lima:

The jurisprudential divergence is well characterized.

The exercise of rural activity by the prosecutor is a controversial matter. It is also controversial the perception by the author of death pension at a value above the minimum wage.

The Turma of Origin recognized the right of the applicant to retirement by rural age for understanding that his condition of special insurance does not discharacterize by the perception of the benefit of retirement by death. It removed the incidence of article 11, § 9th, paragraph I, of Law no. 8.213/91 for considering unconstitutional the section “which value does not exceed the of the smallest benefit of continued benefit of Social Security ”.

Procedure: 0516118-92.2019.4.05.8102 - 2nd TR/EC

[...] It is certain that the perception of values up to a minimum wage is excluded and relativized to grant rural benefits.

According to article 11, § 9, of Law no. 8.213/91, the family member who possesses another source of income is not special insured, except if it is due to:

I – pension benefit for death, accident aid or imprisonment aid, the value of which does not exceed the least benefit of continued benefit of the Social Security;

It is highlighted that here it is not a decision of not recognizing the quality of insurance of the author, simply by the fact of receiving a urban death pension, but by the conclusion that from the value of such a pension, which exceeds the minimum wage, it is that comes the maintenance of the family core in question, thus removing the character of subsistence of rural activity. [...]

Therefore, while the contested judgment considered partially unconstitutional the legal norm cited in the paradigms, these applied the legal device in its entirety.

I am going to analyze the merit.

The solution to paradigms seems to be the right one.

The pension by age to the special insured is provided in article 201, § 7, paragraph II, of the Federal Constitution, which mentions as beneficiaries “the rural workers and for those exercising their activities in the regime of family economy, in these including the rural producer, the garimper and the craftsmanship fisherman”.

As read in the chapter of § 7, the aforementioned retirement is granted by the general social security regime "in the terms of the law".

Therefore, it is up to the ordinary legislator to define the contours of the family economy regime, which was done in detail in paragraph VII and paragraphs of article 11 of Law no. 8.213/91.

In § 1, in particular, the expression "regime of family economy" is defined as "the activity in which the work of the family members is indispensable to the own subsistence and to the socio-economic development of the family core and is exercised in conditions of mutual dependence and cooperation, without the use of permanent employees ".

Therefore, it is essential for the characterization of the system of family economy that the family members extract their maintenance primarily from rural activity exercised in conditions of mutual dependence and collaboration. And it is also, therefore, in the light of this conceptual core that must be interpreted the possibilities of discaracterization of the condition of special insurance provided for in § 9, as follows:

The family member who owns another source of income is not specially insured, except if it is due to:

I – pension benefit by death, accident aid or prison aid, whose value does not exceed the least benefit of continued benefit of the Social Security; (included by Law No. 11.718, of 2008)

II – provision benefit by participation in a complementary provision plan established in accordance with section IV of § 8 of this article; (included by Law No. 11.718, of 2008)

III – exercise of remunerated activity in entressafra or of defense period, not greater than 120 (hundred and twenty) days, running or intercalated, in the calendar year, observed as provided in § 13 of article 12 of Law no. 8.212, of 24 July 1991; (Included by Law no. 11.718, of 2008)

III - exercise of remunerated activity in a period not greater than a hundred and twenty days, running or intercalated, in the calendar year, observed as provided in § 13 of article 12 of Law No. 8.212, of 24 July 1991; (Redacted by Provisory Measure No. 619, of 2013)

III - exercise of remunerated activity in a period not greater than 120 (hundred and twenty) days, running or intercalated, in the calendar year, observed as provided in § 13 of article 12 of Law no. 8.212, of 24 July 1991; (Redirected by Law no. 12.873, of 2013)

IV – exercise of elective mandate of trade union leader of organization of the category of rural workers; (included by Law No. 11,718, of 2008)

V – exercise of mandate of mayor of the Municipality in which it develops the rural activity or of manager of rural cooperative constituted, exclusively, by special insurers, observed the provisions of § 13 of article 12 of Law no. 8.212, of 24 July 1991; (included by Law no. 11.718, of 2008)

VI – partnership or measure granted in the form and conditions established in section I of § 8 of this article; (included by Law No. 11.718, of 2008)

VII – artisanal activity developed with raw material produced by the respective family group, and may be used raw material of other origin, provided that the monthly income obtained in the activity does not exceed the lowest benefit of continued provision of the Social Security; and (Included by Law No. 11.718, 2008)

VIII – artistic activity, provided that in monthly value less than the lowest benefit of continued provision of the Social Security. (included by Law No. 11,718, of 2008) [...]

That is, in the cases listed above, the condition of special insurance is removed because the worker has another source of income sufficient to guarantee his maintenance.

However, as the purpose of maintenance is what characterizes the insurance benefits in general, it is reasonable the provision of paragraph I that makes cease the quality of special insurer to the one who passes to receive a pension benefit by death, accident aid or aid detention in value greater than the least benefit of continued benefit of the Social Security.

Therefore, I have for right the conclusion to which the paradigm of the 2nd Chairman of the Ceará came in this stage of the judgment:

[...] It is highlighted that here it is not a decision of not recognizing the quality of insurance of the author, simply by the fact of receiving pension for urban death and rather by the conclusion that from the value of such pension, which exceeds the minimum wage, it is that comes the maintenance of the family core in question, thus removing the character of subsistence of rural activity. [...]

It is noted that such assertive is in no way contrary to the Summary No. 41/TNU or the Theme No. 532/STJ, since these two precedents deal with the situation of the other members of the family group who have no other source of income. In fact, the strict application of article 11, § 9, paragraph I, of Law No. 8.213/91 does not prevent that the quality of special insurance of the other members of the family is consideredined if and as long as their subsistence still depends on the activity exercised in the regime of family economy.

Finally, it is necessary to draw some considerations on the interpretation given by the Social Security Regulation to the legal device in comment.

Article 9, § 8, incisions I and I-A, of Decree no. 3.048/99 says:

[8] The family member who owns another source of income is not specially insured, except if it is due to: (Redacted by Decree No. 6,722, of 2008).

I - pension benefit by death, accident aid or prison aid, whose value does not exceed that of the least benefit of social security; (Redacted by Decree No. 10.410, of 2020).

I-A - benefit granted to the insured qualified as special insured, regardless of the value; (included by Decree No. 10.410, of 2020). [...]

The paragraph I only repeats the article 11, § 9, paragraph I, of Law no. 8.213/91, but the paragraph I-A introduces a novelty that seems to contradict with paragraph I. Why only the grant of death pension, accident aid and prison aid with a value greater than the least benefit of continued benefit of the Social Security disqualifies the worker as special insured?

The distinction is justified, in my opinion, by a joint interpretation of this disposal with article 15, paragraph I, of Law no. 8.213/91, according to which “heins the quality of insured, independiently of contributions [...] without a deadline, who is in enjoyment of benefit, except aid-accident”.

In fact, it would be contradictory with this legal arrangement that the special insured would lose this quality by receiving insurance benefit just due to this condition, rescued only the case of accident aid.

If the paragraph I takes care of cases in which the benefit perceived or not arises from the condition of insured, but of dependent (pension for death and aid-arrest), or is expressly excluded from the rule of article 15, paragraph I, of Law no. 8.213/91 ( aid-accident).

This is also the interpretation given by the Regulatory Instruction No. 128/2022, which, in its article 112, point IX, subparagraphs "a" and "b" clarifies thatins the quality of special insured the one who perceives (i) "pension benefit by death, aid-accident or aid-reclusion, during the period in which its value does not exceed that of the current minimum wage at the time, considered the value of each benefit when receiving more than one "; or (ii) " benefits whose subsidiary category is the special insured, regardless of the value ".

The amended judgment is in line with this interpretation, as it rejected the author's age-based pension application for the following grounds:

On the retirement by age to rural women, Article 48 of Law No. 8.213/91 prescribes the minimum age of 60 years for men, and 55 years for women, fulfilled the deficiency required in Articles 142 and 143, in the period immediately prior to the claim of the benefit, although in a discontinuous manner.

The author receives Death Pension, offering value above the minimum wage, which discaracterizes his condition of special insurance, in accordance with article 11, § 9, I, of Law 8.213/91.

The case, therefore, is of restoration of the judgment, based on Order Question No. 38:

In the event of a trial on a request for uniformization, the National Court may apply the right to the specific case by resolving the dispute in a definitive manner, provided that the matter is only of law, or, being in fact and of law, does not need to re-examine the proof framework defined by the previous instances, and can therefore restore the judgment disconstitued by the Court of Appeal or Regional Court.

In the case of this, I vote for KNOWING and PROVER the request for national uniformization to restore the sentence of improcedence.

As the appealed judgment is contrary to this understanding, my vote proves the incident, reaffirming the thesis that the condition of special insurance is discharacterized, in accordance with article 11, § 9th, I, of Law 8.213/91, by the perception of death pension of value greater than the minimum wage.

They will have to return to the origin to the purposes of readaptation to the above understanding.

by Voto-Vista

It is an incident of national uniformization of jurisprudence interposed by the INSS against a judgment of the 3rd Turma Recursal of Pernambuco which, denying provision to its appeal, retained the judgment of provision of the pension application by rural age formulated by the author.

The applicant claims, in strict synthesis, that the appealed judgment violates the provisions of article 11, §9th, I, of Law 8.213/91, since the circumstance of the author receiving death pension benefit in the value above the minimum wage removes the condition of special insurance.

The rapporteur voted in order to provide provision to the incident of uniformization interposed, in order to determine the return of the autos to the Turma of origin so that proceeds the judge's adequacy to the understanding signed by this TNU in the judgment of PUIL 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304.

I asked for a better consideration of the case in trial.

With the reporter’s vengeance, I dare to divert.

This is because, the adoption of the norm in question as an absolute criterion for the discaracterization of the condition of special insurance of the rural worker disconsides the socio-economic peculiarities of this class of insured, the differentiated treatment to them secured by the Federal Constitution of 1988 and, even, results in contradictory interpretation with the other hypotheses provided for in article 11, § 9, of Law 8.213/91.

In fact, the provisional rules have a social character and must be interpreted in the light of the constitutional principles that govern the system.

Under this prisma, it seems more reasonable to conclude that, by establishing the criteria laid down in Article 11, §9 of the Benefits Act, the legislator's intention was to keep the special insured in that category as long as he continues to effectively work on the ground and that the exercise of another activity does not make the rural labor economically dispensable.

The interpretative point that must guide the magistrate in the examination of the circumstances indicated in law as disqualifiers of the special insurance condition is the effective dispensability or not of rural work as the main source of sustainability. And there is no other way to perform such analysis except by assessing case-by-case the evidence produced and suppressing the specific circumstances of each situation.

I resume, for example, the situation of a remaining spouse, beneficiary of death pension higher than the minimum for infinitely value, who was left with five children, all under the age of twelve and incapable of helping in the maintenance of the family and/or in the countryside. How can I imagine that in this situation the value of the pension would be sufficient to provide the maintenance of the family core, making rural work discountable?

In the other example, the spouses perceive benefits of rural retirement in the condition of special insured, i.e. the family income is of two minimum wages. The family is composed of husband, wife and four children in school age. How to explain the possibility that this family receives the equivalent to two minimum wages in this situation, and another similar family core will be prevented from offering values resulting from a retirement if the death pension of one of the spouses is slightly higher than the minimum?

Thus, the analysis under the individual perspective of the insured who receives the pension, as proposed in the PUIL 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304 judgment, adopted as reason for the decision by the relator of the present incident, appears incorrect because it discontinue the numerous possibilities in which the family core has no other source of income and depends only and exclusively on the remaining spouse.

Not enough, the understanding that a member of a rural family group beneficiary of a pension for death of a value above the minimum cannot be considered special insured carries unfair discrimination with the various situations described in article 11, §9, of the Benefits Act.

In this sense, we can quote the hypotheses of the incisions III (rewarded activity), IV (selective mandate syndical), V (bearer) and VI (partner/meyer) of §9 of article 11 of the LBPS, situations in which a limit ceiling for the source of extraordinary income was not provided.

And here it is necessary to collate, by the brilliantity of the exposure of arguments, trace of the judgment made by the Court of Appeal of the Tocantins Judicial Section in the judgment of RI 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304, rel. Federal Judge Wagmar Roberto Silva, which well describes the apparent antinomia existing in the device under examination.

[...]6.3. The hypotheses of the incisions III (rewarded activity), IV (seal mandate) and V (reward) of § 9 of article 11 of the LBPS cause disproportionally inequality treatment in relation to the pensionist and artist. The three hypotheses do not limit the source of extraordinary income and reveal that the special insured will maintain its quality of insured even if the reward subsidy, for example, reaches the constitutional ceiling – currently approximately R$18.991.68 – since the reward can vary between 20% to 75% of the subsidy of the state deputy (seal VI of article 29 of the CF).

6.4. The hypothesis of section VI of § 9 of article 11 of LBPS, i.e. the conclusion of partnership or measure granted over up to 50% of the extension of the rural real estate, limited to two tax modules (point I of § 8 of article 11 of LBPS), also destroys the absence of monetary margins to the rural business . In fact, the rural producer (e.g., owner), in a partnership contract, can provide deposits and the partner adhere to the contract with the labour for cultivation in two tax modules.

6.5.Other regulatory discrepancy is contained in the hypothesis of section II of § 9 of article 11 of the LBPS.The special insured keeps its quality even though it perceives provents of pre-financial benefit by participation in complementary pre-financial plan established by the government’s assistant program ( section IV of § 8 of the LBPS).

The confrontation of the hypotheses of § 9 of article 11 of the LBPS concludes that incisions I and VIII had an anti-isonomic treatment without any reasonable ratio for discrimination, specifically as regards the death pension and income of artistic activity – in this case, the coherence should have been temporary, i.e. 120 days run or intercalated each year, to maintain harmony with incisions III.

The consequence of legal discrimination against pensioners, in fact, translates into a legal imposition of giving up the special insured to rural age pension to benefit from the death pension, since it is higher than the minimum wage and that limited benefit.

(RI no. 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304, reporter Wagmar Roberto Silva, 19th Turma 4.0 - adjunta to the Tocantins Recursal Turma, judged on 20/03/2024, full content available for consultation through the link https://pje2g.trf1.jus.br:443/pje/Process/ConsultaDocument/listView.seam?x=24032115574918600000396518609)

The exegese that solves at one time the apparent antinomia described above and addresses the social objectives of the Benefits Act is that which considers the economic criterion provided for in article 11, §9o, I, of Law 8.213/91 only another factor to be analyzed together with the other evidence produced, not by itself only an obstacle to the recognition of the condition of special insurance.

It is noted that the proposed solution is not heterodox, nor is it a novelty in the jurisprudence.

The Supreme Federal Court, in the judgment of Theme 27 of the General Repercussion, declared the inconstitutionality of the objective criterion of miserability adopted by the Organic Law of Social Assistance (RE 567985, rel. Min Gilmar Mendes, Plenum Court, judged on 18/04/2013, published on 03/10/2013), consolidating the jurisprudential understanding that preponder in the analysis of the requirement of miserability the proof set formed and the circumstances of the specific case.

In the same row, the Supreme Court has already settled in trial under the rite of the repeated appeals (Tema 1.115) that the size of rural property does not characterize, by itself, the regime of family economy (REsp no. 1.947.404/RS, reporter Minister Benedito Gonçalves, First Section, judged on 23/11/2022, DJe of 7/12/2022).

Furthermore, in the judgment on Subject 532, the Supreme Court concluded that the urban work of one of the family group members does not discharacter, by itself, the other members as special insured (REsp no. 1.304.479/SP, rapporteur Minister Herman Benjamin, First Section, judged on 10/10/2012, DJe of 19/12/2012).

And finally, it is necessary to clarify the decision made by Minister Benedito Gonçalves in the judgment of REsp 2.121.317/EC, which addressed specifically the issue in debate, in the following terms:

[...] As regards the allegation of infringement to Article 11, § 9, I of Law no. 8.213/1991, the appealed judgment concluded the following (fl. 240, e-STJ):

The [...]

Article 9 of Article 11 of Law No. 8.213/91 provides in the sense of non-qualification as a special insured of the family member who has a death pension greater than the value of a minimum wage.

However, in the specific case, largely in valuation of the other evidence reference, not only of the labor activity, but also, as regards the effective socio-economic condition of the author, who, with the illness, needs, in addition to the daily expenses, other expenses also with his poor health, forced to recognize that the value offered as a pension is not sufficient to the point of dispensing the rural work of the applicant, for which reason must be recognized the right to the perception of his retirement for invalidity as made the judgment.

The fact that the author perceives the husband's death pension, in value slightly above a minimum wage, does not discharacterize his condition of special insurance, since the agricultural activity performed by it, was essential for his subsistence, being him, therefore, due the grant of the pension for rural disability, due to his labor incapacity.

The [...]

The following paragraph of the integrative judgment concerning the question submitted is also examined (p. 270-271, e-STJ):

The [...]

As regards the limitation contained in article 11, §9o, I, of Law 8.213/91, by the amendments it introduced, it is clear that the legislator’s intention was not simply to prohibit that the special insured perceives income from any other type of activity than the agriculture, but rather to establish criteria, in order to keep the rural worker while in that category, provided that he continues effectively working on the ground and that the exercise of another activity does not make the rural labor economically discountable.

The economic criterion should be assessed case by case, according to the specific circumstances of each situation. It would have no sense that the legislator intends to exclude from the classification of special insured a person who receives a benefit of value slightly higher than the minimum salary, while simultaneously authorizing the lease of land, an operation that has the probability of providing income of higher value.

In fact, the legal forecast of disqualification of the special insurance condition of the mortal pension holder of value greater than a minimum wage, should be applied restrictively, since the said value, taking into account the other socio-economic aspects present in each case as well as the effective dispensability or not of rural work as the main source of subsistence.

Ratifying, the non-framework is only justified if the rural worker, due to offering another source of income, can dispose of his own labor in the regime of family economy, which does not constitute the present case, since the embargated only left his rural activities due to the disease that caused him.

(REsp no. 2.121.317, Minister Benedito Gonçalves, DJe of 28/08/2024.)

Following all these considerations, I adhere to the understanding signed by the eminent Federal Judge Lilian Oliveira da Costa Tourinho in divergent vote proclaimed in the judgment of PUIL 1015564-47.2022.4.01.3304/TO, in which he made relevant points on the aspects that should north the analysis of the quality of special insurance in each particular case, such as the number of children, the specific needs of the family group, the characteristics of the region in which rural labor is exercised and the socio-economic aspects in which the members of that group are inserted.

Before all the considerations submitted, in order to uniformize the understanding concerning the subject matter of material law, I propose the establishment of the following legal thesis:

The receipt of death pension in the value above the minimum wage does not characterize, by itself, the quality of the special insurance of the rural worker. The non-framework is only justified if it is proven in the specific case that the earliest income makes the rural labor dispensable for the subsistence and socio-economic development of the family core.
