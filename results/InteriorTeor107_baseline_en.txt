In a second paradigm, the 5th Tower of Rio Grande do Sul also recognized the right to the benefit of the Stock Exchange-Family (Procedure No. 5016603- 17.2024.4.04.7100/RS), concluding that, “although the Executive Power is authorized to compatibilize the number of financial beneficiaries inserted in the PBF with the available budgetary assets (Article 11, § 1, of Law No. 14.601/2023), such authorization does not include, in any way, the establishment of a distinctive criterion based only on the number of members of the family group to be contemplated by the program, measure that clearly violates the principle of isolation.”

The divergence on legal interpretation is evident: while for the 2nd Court of Appeal of Pernambuco there would be no possibility of syndicating the administrative decisions relating to the inclusion/exclusion of beneficiaries in the aid program relating to the Family Stock Exchange by the Judiciary, especially due to the need for compatibility with the available budgetary assets, for the 5th Court of Appeal of Rio Grande do Sul, in similar procedures, the aid benefit should be granted to the cases that properly fit into the legal requirements, and there is no place for the discretion of the Public Administration.

The application for uniformization of the interpretation of the law filed by a beneficiary excluded from the Borsa-Family Program, against a judgment of the 2nd Tower of Recursal of Pernambuco which gave provision to the Union appeal to judge improcedent the request for restoration of the assistance benefit, on the basis of the absence of subjective right to receipt, in the face of the budgetary limitation provided for in article 11, §1, of Law no. 14.601/2023.

The question in question consists in defining whether the fulfillment of the legal requirements of the Scholarship-Family Program guarantees the subjective right to benefit, even in cases where the interested party integrates a single-person family and the municipality where it resides has already exceeded the percentage limit of 16% established by Portaria MDS no 911/2023.

The appealed judgment argued that the granting of the benefit is subject to the public administration’s discretion to compatibilize the social policy with the available budgetary assets, without having the applicant’s subjective right. The paradigms mentioned affirm the subjective right to receiving the Family Stock Exchange as long as the legal criteria are met, rejecting the validity of administrative limitations based exclusively on the family composition or quotas fixed in ministerial portaries, against the principle of isonomy.

The matter reveals multiplier potential, facing the high number of unipersonal families and the percentage limit imposed by the infralegal standard, the incident must be affected as a representative of controversy, in accordance with article 16 of the Internal Reglement of the National Cluster of Uniformation.
