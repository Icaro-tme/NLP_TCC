It is a request for uniformization of interpretation of federal law (PEDILEF) interposed by the author and addressed to the National Court of Uniformization of Jurisprudence of the Federal Special Judges (TNU), against judgment of the 10th Court of Appeal of São Paulo, which gave provision to the unnamed appeal interposed by the INSS, in order to remove the period of special labor exercised from 13/01/1997 to 31/12/2016, in which the author part worked as “refratarist worker” and “chairman I”, exposed occasionally and intermittently to the chemical agent benzen – event 1, ACOR8 .

In its grounds, the author considers the occurrence of discrepancy of understanding between the appealed judgment and the PEDILEF no. 0500667-18.2015.4.05.8312, Federal Court Relator Gisele Chaves Sampaio Alcântara, DJe 23/02/2017, according to which the measurement in the work environment (qualitative analysis) of exposure to the chemical agent dust of silica has been dismissed because it is a recognizably carcinogenic element in humans, according to LINACH, Group 1, with registration in the Chemical Abstract Service - CAS no. 014808-60-7 event 1, OUT9 .

In addition, it claims that there is no secure level of exposure to the harmful agent benzen, so that the quality exposure already guarantees you the right to the conversion of the special working period, because it is a cancer agent, reasons for which it postulates the admission of the federal uniformization application. The PEDILEF was not admitted in the origin ( event 1, DECADMPU13 ) and, after interposition of internal aggravate, it was admitted by the Presidency of the National Uniformization Tower ( event 4, DESPADEC1 ).

The request for uniformization of the interpretation of federal law is provided in Article 14 of Law No. 10.259/2001, being acceptable when “there is a divergence between decisions on material law matters made by Turmas Recursais in the interpretation of the law ”. For matters of material law, the disputed points of law, i.e., those alleging to the construction, from the statements of the normative texts, of the legal norm of the specific case, provided that, for the discontinuation of the controversy, it is not necessary the re-evaluation of evidence or the re-examination of the facts specifically discussed in the claim.

In order to demonstrate the divergence, it is necessary the confrontation of the appealed judgment with the paradigm judgment of the Recursal (or Regional) Turma of a different region, of the TNU itself or of the STJ (Article 14, § 4o). It is also possible that it is used, for such purposes, the summary enunciation of the TNU or of the STJ. In relation to the specific object of the appeal , versa the present incident on the possibility of conversion of the special labor period in common in face of the exposure of the author to the harmful agent, in an eventual and intermittent way, for the purpose of review of the pension benefit for the time of contribution of which the owner is from 08/07/2019.

Federal Judge CAIO MOYSÉS DE LIMA: This is an action moved by MENEZIO VIEIRA DE LIMA in front of the INSTITUTO NATIONAL DO SOCIAL SEGURO - INSS, which has the object of the review of retirement for time of contribution, from the date of entry of the administrative request (DER), on 08/07/2019, through the computing of special activity exercised in the period from 13/01/1997 to 31/12/2016.

The judgment considered partially proceeding the request, condemning the defendant to (i) averbar, as special service time, the period from 13/01/1997 to 31/12/2016; (ii) review the retirement for the author's contribution time, from 08/07/2019 (DIB); and (iii) pay the accumulated differences, in accordance with the Manual of Procedures for the Calculations in the Federal Justice, in force at the time of the judge's execution.

The defendant applies, in synthesis, that (i) the exposure to the chemical agent is given occasional and intermittent; (ii) the fact that the assessment of the benzen is qualitative, does not imply the unnecessary to identify its concentration in the work environment; (iii) in the case of the autos it has not been proven that the presence of the benzen in the work environment in concentration exceeds the detection limit, the exposure is infime and irrelevant for the purposes of occupational hygiene; and (iv) the profysiography is incompatible with the exposure to the benzen, in the absence of usuality and permanence.

From the entry into force of Law No. 9.032/95 it passed to require the usuality and permanence of exposure to the harmful agent. Thus, it is necessary to say case by case, based on the description of the activity exercised by the insured, if the exposure to the harmful agent constituted an intrinsic and indissociable aspect of the exercise of the said activity. In this case, in the controversial period, the author exercised the activities of “refratarist worker” and “dealer I”, so described in the PPP : The document expressly mentions that “the work exercised in the area referred to the chemical agent Benzeno, is executed occasional and intermittent” .

Therefore, it is noted that the exposure to the benzen was occasional, i.e. it was not indissociable from the duties of the author's office, which prevents the recognition of the activity as special. In front of the above, voting for giving provision to the appeal of the party re, to judge improcedent the action. Without condemnation in fees, because only the defeated applicant must arca with the subsequent payments, in accordance with article 55 of the Law no 9.099/95, combined with article 1 of the Law no 10.259/2001.

In its grounds, the author considers the occurrence of discrepancy of understanding between the appealed judgment and the PEDILEF no. 0500667-18.2015.4.05.8312, Federal Court Relator Gisele Chaves Sampaio Alcântara, DJe 23/02/2017, according to which the measurement in the work environment (qualitative analysis) of exposure to the chemical agent dust of silica has been dismissed because it is a recognizably carcinogenic element in humans, according to LINACH, Group 1, with registration in the Chemical Abstract Service - CAS no. 014808-60-7 event 1, OUT9 .

In addition, it claims that there is no secure level of exposure to the harmful agent benzen, so that the quality exposure already guarantees you the right to the conversion of the special labor period, because it is a cancer agent, reasons for which it postulates the admission of the federal uniformization application. From the beginning, in relation to the content of the paradigm judgment, it is to be noted that there was no necessary analytical quotation between the appealed judge and the understanding of the TNU in the appeal part by the author, circumstance that attracts the application of article 14, V, 'c', of the RITNU.

The appealing party was limited to reference to PEDILEF no. 0500667-18.2015.4.05.8312, in which the work environment measurement (qualitative analysis) of exposure to the chemical agent silica powder was dismissed because it was recognized as a cancerogen element in humans, according to LINACH, Group 1, with registration in Chemical Abstract Service - CAS no. 014808-60-7, without performing the appropriate analytical quotation, without comparing its terms and foundations with those adopted by the appeal, in order to raise the controversy.

In this point, it should be noted that it is not enough to simply indicate the alleged dissidence in the body of the text of the Application for Uniformation without the applicant demonstrating, in a detailed and analytical way, that for the same factual situation have been given different interpretations or legal solutions. In the case, I observe that the appealing author party did not discontinue from carrying out the appropriate analytical quotation that allows to verify factic-juridical similarity between the appealed judgment and the paradigm judgment of TNU. There was no examination and confrontation of the factic and legal situation contained in the judgment quoted with the now combated judgment, there was merely transcription of traces.

In addition, proceeding to the analytical quotation between the judge of origin and the paradigm pointed in this recursal path, there is also the absence of factic and legal similarity between the appealed judgment and the collated arrest in terms of the controversial issue in the autos. It is seen that the appealed judgment distracted the speciality of labor in face of the eventual and intermittent exposure of the author’s part, in the exercise of the post of “refratarist trainer” and “chairman I”, to the harmful agent benzeno , situation that does not amolate to the paradigm judgment collated in the recursal reasons (event 1, OUT9 ), which seen, in that specific case, was analyzed the exposure to the harmful poe (mineral poe) that the agroperian activity exercised .

In this context, it is verified that the author has not presented valid paradigm to prove the divergence with the judgment objurgated in matter of material law, so little has proven the factical and legal similarity between the processes in analysis, not therefore meeting the requirements of article 14 of Law no. 10.259/2001. Additionally, it should be highlighted that the Recursal Tower of origin, through sovereign analysis of the proof asset, concluded that the author’s intermittent exposure to the chemical agent benzen cannot be considered special after 29/04/1995, faced with the survival of Law no. 9.032/95, being indispensable the permanent, non occasional nor intermittent exposure to the harmful agent, that the activity is qualified as special.

This time, the analysis of the proof acquis produced in the fact, in which it consists in the description of the activities carried out by the author in the use of benzen, as well as the need to produce proof regarding the specification of the harmful agents to which he was exposed in the performance of the labor as "refratarist worker" and "member I", is a matter that refuses to the limits from request for uniformization, in the express terms of Summary 42: No one is known of uniformization incident that involves reexamination of fact matter.
