It is a complaint filed by Eusamar de Lima Abreu against a judgment of the 1st Court of Appeal of Maranhão, in the cases of proceedings no. 1011522-62.2021.4.01.3700, under the claim of non-compliance of a decision made by TNU in Representative Theme no. 177 . The complainant party claimed to have obtained a favourable judgment to the grant of aid-sickness in the previous proceedings, with determination of maintenance of the benefit for the minimum period of 12 months.

The new application for the restoration of the benefit was considered improced by the Court of Appeal, which motivated the present complaint, under the argument that the decision would contradict the thesis established in Theme 177 of the TNU, as regards professional rehabilitation and the constatation of partial and permanent incapacity. II. QUESTION IN DISCUSSION The question in question is whether the Court of Appeal ruled that the National Court of Uniformation ruled on Theme 177 to justify the use of the complaint as an adequate means to ensure the authority of the decision of the Court of Appeal.

In accordance with Article 40 of the Internal Rules of the TNU, the complaint has the capacity to preserve the competence or guarantee the authority of the decisions of the National Trial of Uniformation . However, in accordance with Article 41, I, of the same diploma, no complaint is made when there is no decision of the TNU in the original proceedings . The precedents signed in representative matters do not have binding (exprocessual) effect on the other court bodies, having only persuasive character . In the specific case, there is no in the original proceedings any decision of the TNU that has been discontinued , nor has there been an order of adequacy of the Judge of the Turma Recursal to the undertaken subject 177.

The complainant party did not submit an application for uniformization against the ruling of the Court of Appeal, which would be the appropriate procedural means. The complaint cannot be used as a recursal succession. In the face of the absence of the end-processual decision of TNU in the origin proceedings, the complaint is unable . IV. DISPOSITIVE AND TESE Unknown complaint, with indeference of the initial petition , for the absence of the decision of TNU in the origin proceedings.
